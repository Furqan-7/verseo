Everything you need
to know

Questions about how CreatorOS reads, scores and acts on your business.

CreatorOS is an AI operating system for creators. It links your brand deals, contracts, deadlines, content performance and payments, then turns them into decisions and actions: risk scores, tasks, reminders, follow-ups and recommendations.

Deal Health is calculated by rules: financial terms are 25%, deadlines 25%, contract terms 30% and scope 20%. The AI explains the score using the contract clauses, but the score itself comes from code, so it is consistent and auditable.

No. CreatorOS prepares drafts and events, and nothing is sent or booked until you approve it. The flow is always AI prepares → creator approves → system executes.

CreatorOS never invents deadlines. If a deliverable has no explicit date, the task is marked "needs your date" until you confirm one.
